How this tool works

UAE Corporate Tax is generally applied at 9% on profits above AED 375,000. This tool shows a clean breakdown:

  • Profit: what you enter
  • Tax-free portion: up to the threshold
  • Taxable income: profit − threshold (if positive)
  • Tax payable: taxable income × tax rate
