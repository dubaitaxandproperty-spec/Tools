How This UAE Corporate Tax Calculator Works

This UAE corporate tax calculator uses a simplified educational formula. It starts with your annual revenue, deducts allowable business expenses, then applies any additional tax adjustments to estimate taxable income.

Under this model, the first AED 375,000 of taxable income is treated at 0%. Any taxable income above AED 375,000 is estimated at 9%. The tool then shows your estimated tax payable, effective tax rate, and post-tax profit.

Final UAE tax treatment can vary based on legal status, exemptions, transfer pricing, qualifying free zone income, natural person rules, and applicable guidance.

Who Should Use This Tool

This tool is useful for UAE companies, mainland businesses, free zone businesses, consultants, freelancers, sole proprietors, natural persons carrying on business activities, and Indian founders evaluating potential UAE tax exposure before formal structuring.
